Viel's very cheesy pizza with accurate measurement shop

Add slices, drag the knife, and watch every slice's radius, arc, angle and area respond.

Visual

Click a label on the pizza (r, θ, A or s) to see how it is solved. Drag the knife around its slice to resize it, pull it out of the pizza to detach it, then drop it near either cut edge of any slice to attach it there. The knife always points at the pizza's center. Select the ✋ hand (top right of the visual) to drag slices around and drop them back on the pizza. To texture the pizza, save your picture as image.png in the same folder as this file.
